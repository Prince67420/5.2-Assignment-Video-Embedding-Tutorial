F Field Notes / Web basics LESSON 05.02

A practical walkthrough

Put a YouTube video
on your page.

Take a video from YouTube, copy its embed code, and add it to your own HTML. Follow the five steps, then check the finished player below.

5 stepsAbout 5 minutesHTML

The process

Work down the list. Keep the YouTube tab open while you edit your page.

01

Choose a video

Open the video on YouTube

Go to YouTube and open the video you want to show. Make sure it is appropriate for your audience and that its owner allows embedding.

02

Open sharing tools

Select Share

Under the video player, choose Share. A panel of sharing options will appear.

03

Get the HTML

Choose Embed

In the sharing panel, select Embed. YouTube will show an <iframe> snippet for the video.

04

Copy the player code

Copy the <iframe>

Use the Copy button next to the code. Copy the full snippet, including the opening and closing iframe tags.

05

Add it to your page

Paste, save, and test

Open your HTML file and paste the iframe where the player should appear, inside the <body>. Save the file, open the page in a browser, and check that the video plays.
